Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to PocketLLM Lite ("the App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience using our App.

PocketLLM Lite is developed and maintained by Prashant C. By using this App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

PocketLLM Lite is local-first. The application contains no advertising SDK, behavioral analytics package, or remote crash reporter, but optional features can contact destinations you select.

  • Local Data: Chats, settings, imported documents, indexes, memories, notes, and local diagnostic logs are stored in the application sandbox.
  • Local Measurements: The App can display device RAM, storage, ABI, CPU-core, battery, and thermal information locally. It does not automatically upload those measurements.
  • No Developer Analytics: The App does not include behavioral analytics, advertising, or remote crash-reporting SDKs.

3. How We Use Your Information

Data stored by the App is used to provide the features you request:

  • Provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • Persist chat, memory, document, prompt, persona, skill, and preference state
  • Generate local diagnostics that you may inspect or export
  • Send only the request content needed by a provider or optional action you invoke

4. Data Storage and Security

Your conversation data and AI model settings are stored locally on your device. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect any information we do collect. However, please note that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

Optional connections include GitHub release checks and skill files, Hugging Face model search/download, Tavily search, configured Ollama or OpenAI-compatible providers, and links you open. A remote provider may receive model identifiers, prompts, relevant history, attachments, or embedding text needed for that request. Platform speech and text-to-speech engines may also have their own network behavior. Review each service's terms and privacy policy before use.

6. Advertising

PocketLLM Lite v1.0.38 contains no advertising SDK or ad display integration.

7. Children's Privacy

PocketLLM Lite is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Inspect, edit, export, or delete local application data through available controls
  • Disable optional network features or enable application-level Strict Offline Mode
  • Delete application data through Android settings or by uninstalling the App

9. Strict Offline boundary

Strict Offline Mode blocks non-loopback application-owned HTTP/Dio requests and policy-aware HTTP(S) link handoffs. Loopback remains available. It is not a device firewall and cannot control another app after you intentionally hand an action to it, such as an email draft or package installation.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
