# Community Code of Conduct

PocketLLM Lite welcomes contributors of every experience level. Participate
with curiosity, patience, and respect.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code, not a person's identity or worth.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while still addressing harm.
- Respect privacy and do not publish someone else's personal information.
- Keep project spaces free from harassment, threats, sexualized attention,
  discrimination, and sustained disruption.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when reviewing or recommending work.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **prashantc592114@gmail.com**. Include the
relevant link, what happened, and any context that can help review the report.
Reports will be handled as privately as practical.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, limit participation, or ban a
participant when necessary to protect the community. Good-faith technical
disagreement is welcome; abusive behavior is not.
